# Ignis

Ignis is a single-player 2D narrative platformer developed in Construct 3 for Games Fleadh 2026.

The game combines platforming, puzzle-solving, light-based mechanics and narrative elements, with inspiration from Slavic folklore.

The original concept for Ignis was created by me, and I was responsible for all of the artwork and visual assets used throughout the game. The project was then developed collaboratively as part of a team.

## About the Game

The player takes control of Ignis, who begins trapped inside a house after his parents leave to investigate a disturbance involving the weakening Mother Flame.

After finding a hidden key and escaping the house, the player progresses through several environments while learning movement mechanics, avoiding enemies and navigating increasingly dangerous areas.

Ignis is accompanied throughout the game by a small dog companion.

The player's flame represents their life and plays an important role both visually and mechanically within the game.

## Gameplay Features

- 2D platform movement
- Jumping and crouching
- Interactive environments
- Key-based progression
- Dynamic light and flame system
- Dog companion system
- Enemy and damage mechanics
- Health and lives system
- Collectibles
- Cinematic chase sequence
- Puzzle-solving
- Multiple gameplay areas and cutscenes
- Game Over system
- Narrative cliffhanger ending

## Folklore Inspiration

One of the game's major sequences involves the player being chased by a large animated house with legs.

This was inspired by Baba Yaga and Slavic folklore.
